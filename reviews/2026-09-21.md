## Evaluation evidence

### Newly published

[MathArena](https://matharena.ai/) announced DeepSeek V4.1 Flash results on September 18 and Astra/Fable low-effort results on September 19. We transcribed the observed May, June and August ArXivMath/BrokenArXiv accuracy and confidence ranges, not the IRT-based Expected Performance or mixed-month Overall figures.

- [Astra low](https://matharena.ai/models/openai_gpt_6_astra_low): Codex 0.153.3; OpenRouter/OpenAI only; low effort; 1,050,000 context.
- [Fable low](https://matharena.ai/models/anthropic_fable_51_low): Claude 2.1.267; Anthropic only; low effort; 128,000 maximum output.
- [DeepSeek max](https://matharena.ai/models/deepseek_deepseek_v41_flash): DeepCode 0.3.1; Fireworks only; max effort; 384,000 maximum tokens; temperature/top-p 1; 4 CPU/12GB.

Each model/harness has its own cohort, no combined ranking. Actual run dates and complete allowed-tool inventories are not disclosed, so remain unknown. Dataset months are not evaluation dates. The source's model release metadata is not used to overwrite vendor launch dates.

[OpenAI's legal-system comparison](https://openai.com/index/astra-for-law/) reports 54.0% versus 38.7% overall correctness on 200 private validation US legal questions, both at highest reasoning effort. Full Law configuration and web-search-only Astra are separate cohorts because tools/instructions differ. Not the public full Legal Research Bench; not an independent base-model ranking.
