## Evaluation provenance and conditions

- [Artificial Analysis change log](https://artificialanalysis.ai/changelog): September 21 Grok 4.7 and MiMo Pro, September 22 Sol, Luna and Opus 5.5. [Methodology](https://artificialanalysis.ai/evaluations/artificial-analysis-intelligence-index) was revised to **v4.3.2** on September 19. Index scores are points, not percentages; 20 variant rows are an excerpt, so no official all-model rank is claimed. Opus variants use Anthropic's default fallback. Other detailed tool allowances and actual execution dates remain unknown. Example max-effort scores: Opus 5.5 **58**, Sol **48**, Luna **37**; Grok xhigh **46**, MiMo Pro default **46**. These are recorded as different effort variants.
- [OpenAI release table](https://openai.com/index/introducing-gpt-6-sol-and-luna/) reports Sol 33.2% on **AutomationBench 1.0.6 xhigh**, 68.8% on **DeepSWE v1.1 max**, 56.4% on **Agents' Last Exam V1 max**, and 60.5% on **OSWorld 2.0 offline v2026.08.08 xhigh partial reward**; Luna has 66.6% on **DeepSWE v1.1 max**. The OSWorld subset is a separate metric.
- [Anthropic's Opus 5.5 table](https://www.anthropic.com/claude-opus-5-5) reports 66.4% on **Terminal-Bench 4.0 xhigh** (Claude Code, 5 trials/task), 54.4% on **FrontierCode 1.1 Main max**, 57.8% on **CursorBench 4.0 max** and 58.7% on **Terminal-Bench-Science 0.1 max** (Claude Code, 3 trials/task). Safeguard fallback is noted in the announcement; effort and tool setup differ from other providers' rows.
- [SpaceXAI's Grok table](https://x.ai/news/grok-4-7) reports 46.3% CursorBench 4.0 xhigh, 71.0% DeepSWE 1.1 **high** (marked with an asterisk in the source), 37.6% Terminal-Bench 4.0 xhigh. Its DeepSWE high and other xhigh scores are not treated as the same reasoning budget.
- [Xiaomi Pro](https://huggingface.co/XiaomiMiMo/MiMo-V2.6-Pro-RL) / [Flash](https://huggingface.co/XiaomiMiMo/MiMo-V2.6-Flash-RL) model cards publish DeepSWE v1.1 (71.9/67.9), AutomationBench v1.0.6 (53.1/52.3), Terminal-Bench 4.0 (34.9/28.8), and Toolathlon-Verified (76.9/73.6). The cards do not fully specify harness, tool set, effort or run date, so rows remain provider-reported and rank-excluded.
- [Xiaomi 9B card](https://huggingface.co/XiaomiMiMo/MiMo-V2.6-Distill-Qwen-9B) reports **61.1% SWE-bench Verified avg@3** and **37.1% Terminal-Bench 2.1 avg@1**. These are specific to the distilled SFT checkpoint and are separate metrics from other SWE-bench and Terminal-Bench editions.
